# Mwanainchi Market & Kitchen

Build the Mwanainchi 2.0 website and food ecommerce ecosystem according to the attached specification document (MWANAINCHI 2.0 — COMPLETE ECOMMERCE, RESTAURANT & DELIVERY MANAGEMENT ECOSYSTEM). Implement the premium brand identity (Charcoal #171A18, Cream #F8F5EF, Orange #F47A35, Natural Green #557A46), complete homepage, butchery catalogue with weight-based (kg/g) meat cuts and prep preferences, "Our Kitchen" restaurant ordering with meal customisations, responsive mixed shopping cart, multi-step checkout with delivery zones and M-Pesa/card/cash payment options, customer account portals, and real-time order tracking.
